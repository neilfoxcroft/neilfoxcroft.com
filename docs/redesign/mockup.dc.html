// about

I'm a backend engineer at Entelect with 4.5+ years building Java and Kotlin Spring Boot microservices for banking and retail financial services. Most of my work sits where systems meet: the APIs, integrations and orchestration that let digital channels, partners and point-of-sale systems talk to each other.

Most recently, at Shoprite Financial Services, I owned the electricity supplier integrations for Namibia and Botswana end to end, from requirements and partner calls through release and production support. Before that I spent two years on value-added services at Standard Bank.

Away from the keyboard I climb, hike (most recently the whole Pacific Crest Trail), am learning to paint Warhammer miniatures, and tinker with a homelab.
